# Level 02 — “Crosswind Banter”
**Theme:** Steady crosswind + chatty passengers  
**Duration:** ~5 min

## Setup
- Crosswind pushes plane right; Navigator must call corrections
- Cabin: passenger demands drinks mid-approach (blocks aisle)

## Objectives
- Maintain centerline with verbal wind calls
- Cabin serves or straps passenger before 1000 ft
- Land

## New systems unlocked
- Wind vector on Navigator HUD
- Cabin “distract” mini-interaction

## Failures
- Drift off runway, cabin panic from ignored passenger

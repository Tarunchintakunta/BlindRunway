# Level 04 — “Fog & Fibs”
**Theme:** Low visibility. Instruments partially lie for 8 seconds bursts.  
**Duration:** ~6 min

## Setup
- Fog; Pilot sees almost nothing
- Navigator instruments glitch (amber) — must say “IGNORE ALT” during glitch
- Cabin: emergency light flicker + one passenger panic spiral

## Objectives
- Survive two glitch windows without Pilot trusting bad numbers
- Calm passenger (Cabin) during second glitch
- Land on ILS

## Design note
Teaches distrust + confirmation culture. Hilarious blame screen if Pilot obeyed glitch.

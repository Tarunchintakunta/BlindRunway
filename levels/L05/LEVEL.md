# Level 05 — “Short Final Circus”
**Theme:** Boss tutorial. Everything at once, still fair.  
**Duration:** ~7–8 min

## Setup
- Short runway, light rain, mild crosswind, 6 passengers, 2 crates, 1 small cabin fire event
- Fuel tight (Navigator must call fuel once)

## Objectives
- Gear + flaps correct
- Extinguish fire
- Secure ≥1 crate
- Panic < 80%
- Stop inside short gate box

## Win
Unlocks “Campaign Clear — Act I” badge; teaser card for Levels 6–30 “Coming soon routes”

## UI finale
Route map lights up 5 airport nodes; camera pulls to globe silhouette
